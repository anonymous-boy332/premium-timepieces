Selected watches may include warranty coverage. Please check the product information or contact our support team for warranty-related questions.

05 — CARE

Watch Care

Proper care can help keep your timepiece looking elegant and performing reliably for longer.

  • Avoid unnecessary contact with water.
  • Keep the watch away from harsh chemicals.
  • Store it safely when not in use.
  • Clean with a soft, dry cloth.
