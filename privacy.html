Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Premium Timepieces, we value your trust and are committed to keeping your personal information safe. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our website or place an order with us.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website, contact us or place an order, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us.

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping and billing information
  • Order and transaction details
  • Messages and information submitted through our contact forms

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information only when necessary to operate and improve our services.

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Arrange shipping and delivery
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Send important order updates
  • Improve our website and shopping experience
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized activity

3. Payment Information

Payment information is handled according to the payment method selected during checkout.

We aim to use trusted payment providers and appropriate security measures when processing online payments. We do not intentionally store complete payment card details on our website.

Important: Never send your card number, CVV, password or other sensitive payment credentials through email, WhatsApp or contact forms.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use cookies, browser storage or similar technologies to provide essential functionality and improve your shopping experience.

For example, shopping cart information may be temporarily stored in your browser so that your selected products remain available while you shop.

5. Order Information

When you place an order, we may retain information related to that order for customer service, delivery, accounting, security and record-keeping purposes.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share limited information with trusted service providers when necessary to complete an order or provide a requested service, such as delivery, payment processing or technical services.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for functions such as payment processing, analytics, hosting, authentication, communications or delivery.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Your Choices

Depending on the information we hold about you, you may contact us to request information about your personal data or ask us to correct inaccurate information.

You may also choose not to provide certain information, although this may prevent us from processing an order or providing some services.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology or legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
